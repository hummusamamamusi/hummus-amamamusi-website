A FEW WORDS ABOUT US

Hummus Amamamusi is a cozy, traditional spot in the heart of Kraków, where a relaxed atmosphere meets a kitchen full of fresh, plant-based flavors. We serve vegetarian and vegan dishes, and our team will gladly help you find something that matches your taste. It's a great place for a quick breakfast, brunch, lunch, or a casual get-together in a good atmosphere.

All of our dishes are made from fresh ingredients that we prepare on site. You'll also find great coffee, matcha, kombucha, teas, lemonades, and freshly squeezed juices.

If you're planning an event or would like us to bring catering to your home, let us know. Hummus is a perfect choice for large parties, buffets, or anyone on the go.

Come visit us and try it for yourself!

A FEW WORDS ABOUT OUR FOOD

We serve the best vegetarian, vegan and hummus dishes in Kraków! Every item on the menu is available all day, so you can order whatever you like, whenever you like.

In our restaurant we celebrate plant-based cooking, full of fresh vegetables, aromatic spices and Middle Eastern inspiration. Our menu was created for people looking for vegetarian and vegan food in Kraków — from creamy hummus and homemade pickles, through falafel and tabbouleh, to vegetable-packed mezze bowls. It is a kitchen full of color, freshness and bold flavors, where chickpeas, tahini, fresh herbs, olive oil, sumac and za'atar take center stage.

To start the day, we offer breakfasts in Kraków that you can enjoy all day long. On the menu you will find shakshuka, scrambled eggs on butter brioche with parmesan and chili oil, a breakfast burrito with egg, cheddar, avocado and sriracha, as well as vegetarian and vegan menemen. If you feel like something more classic, choose pita with hummus and pickles. Our breakfasts combine Middle Eastern cuisine with a modern take on plant-based cooking, so they work equally well for vegetarians and people on a vegan diet.

We are very proud of the delicious food we serve you every day at Hummus Amamamusi. We would love for you to come and see for yourself that we are absolutely right!
