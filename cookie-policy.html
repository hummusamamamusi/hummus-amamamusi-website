This policy explains how the website hummus-amamamusi.pl (the “Service”) uses cookies and similar technologies in connection with Hummus Amamamusi restaurant.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data processed via analytics cookies is Hummus Amamamusi, Beera Meiselsa 4, 31-058 Kraków, Poland, e-mail: amamamusi@gmail.com, phone: +48 533 306 288.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They can remember preferences or help measure how a site is used.

3. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary – required for basic operation, including storing your cookie choice in the browser (localStorage). These do not require consent.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4) – help us understand how visitors use the Service. They are activated only after you accept them in the cookie banner.

We do not use marketing or advertising cookies. Google Analytics is configured with IP anonymisation and without Google advertising signals.

4. Google Analytics

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). The measurement script loads only after you give consent.

Data may be processed on Google servers, including outside the European Economic Area. Google may rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and other GDPR-compliant safeguards. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Legal basis and purpose

Analytics cookies are processed on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable ePrivacy rules) to measure traffic and improve the Service.

6. Retention

  • Your cookie choice – until removed from your browser (localStorage).
  • Google Analytics cookies – as defined by Google (typically up to 14 months, depending on cookie type).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, object, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Poland: PUODO.

8. Managing your consent

On your first visit we show a banner with “Cookie settings” and “Accept”. In settings you can enable or disable analytics cookies and save your choice with “Accept selected”. You can change your choice at any time via cookie settings in the site footer or by clearing site data in your browser.
