This policy explains how the website hummus-amamamusi.pl (the “Service”) uses cookies and similar technologies in connection with Hummus Amamamusi restaurant.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data processed via analytics cookies is Hummus Amamamusi, Beera Meiselsa 4, 31-058 Kraków, Poland, e-mail: amamamusi@gmail.com, phone: +48 533 306 288.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They can remember preferences or help measure how a site is used.

3. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary – required for basic operation, including storing your cookie choice in localStorage, or in sessionStorage when local storage is unavailable. These do not require consent.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4) – help us understand how visitors use the Service. Analytics cookies are stored only after you consent, either with “Accept” or with “Accept selected” while the analytics switch is on.

We do not store marketing cookies and we do not enable ad personalisation. The Google tag loads on every visit in advanced Consent Mode v2. If you decline, Google Analytics 4 receives only anonymous, cookieless signals. Google Analytics is configured with IP anonymisation and without Google advertising signals.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), measurement ID: G-2MFMP6783S. Its cookies are stored after you consent. Without consent, only anonymous cookieless signals are sent.

Data may be processed on Google servers, including outside the European Economic Area. Google may rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and other GDPR-compliant safeguards. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Legal basis and purpose

Analytics cookies are processed on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 399 of the Polish Act of 12 July 2024 – the Electronic Communications Law) to measure traffic and improve the Service.

6. Retention

  • Your cookie choice – until removed from your browser. It is stored in localStorage, or in sessionStorage until the end of the browser session when local storage is unavailable.
  • Google Analytics cookies – as defined by Google (typically up to 14 months, depending on cookie type).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, object, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Poland: PUODO.

8. Managing your consent

On your first visit a banner appears at the bottom of the page and the “Cookie settings” dialog opens at the same time.

The banner has two buttons:

  • Accept — turns analytics cookies on, stores your consent and closes both the banner and the settings dialog.
  • Cookie settings — opens the settings dialog. On a first visit that dialog is already open.

In the settings dialog the “Analytics cookies” switch starts turned on. Accept selected stores the current state of the switch: on means you consent to analytics cookies, off means you do not. Closing the dialog with the cross, by clicking the dimmed background, or with the Escape key does not store a choice. The banner stays until you pick one of these options.

Your choice is stored in the browser in localStorage (key restaurant_cookie_consent, value granted or denied). If localStorage is unavailable, it is stored in sessionStorage and lasts only until you close the tab or browser window.

You can change your choice at any time via cookie settings in the footer of the home page and the English version, on the 404 page, and in this policy. The same dialog opens. If the browser has no stored choice yet, the banner is shown again as well. You can also remove the choice by clearing this site’s data in your browser. If you decline, the Service deletes Google Analytics cookies stored for this domain.
